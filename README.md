# Practical_Application_1
Coupon Acceptance Analysis
In this repository, I apply data analysis, plotting, statistical summarization, and data visualization skills and techniques to a machine learning problem.

Overview

	The task is to answer to the question
“Will a customer accept the coupon?” The goal of this project is to apply visualizations and probability distributions to identify between customers who accepted a driving coupon versus those who did not.   To complete this task I worked with Jupiter notebook locally

Data

	“This data is from the UCI Machine Learning Repository and was collected via a survey on Amazon Mechanical Turk. The survey describes different driving scenarios, including the destination, current time, weather, and passenger, and then asks people whether they will accept the coupon if they are the driver. “

Deliverables

	To analyze  the data,I use my knowledge of pandas and Python to create statistical analysis to find differences in customers who accepted and rejected the coupon. I also  did use Seaborn libraries to create a visualization using Python. 

Report

	In conclusion, drivers who go to bars more than once a month show significantly higher acceptance rates. This suggests that familiarity with bar environments strongly predicts coupon acceptance. Regular bar-goers likely see immediate value in bar coupons
Another important factor that influence in the customer decision are people age, drivers under 30 show higher acceptance rates because they have flexible schedules for bar visits and they are more price sensitive and coupon motivated

	The Social Context Influences Decisions. Drivers without kids as passengers are more likely to accept, the presence of adult passengers might actually encourage bar coupon acceptance

	Another important variable that influence people coupon 
acceptance are people income, low income drivers(especially those who frequent cheap restaurants) show higher acceptance. This suggest that coupons provide meaningful savings for budget consciousness consumers.
